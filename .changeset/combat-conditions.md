- Added the Core Rulebook combat conditions as one-click token statuses: Prone, Pinned, Grappled, Helpless, Blinded, Deafened, Blood Loss, Unaware and Surprised — apply from the token status HUD, with their book test penalties (−10 prone, −20 pinned, −30 blinded) feeding into every test's visible modifier breakdown while active.
- Statuses toggled from the token HUD now carry their test penalty through the modifier funnel, so no condition applies a silent modifier.
