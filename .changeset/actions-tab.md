"rogue-trader": patch
---

- Added an Actions tab to the character sheet: all 31 of the Core Rulebook's combat actions (Table 9-4) as two-column chips in the skills screen's style, sourced from the new actions compendium pack.
- Chips grey out when an action's printed prerequisite is not met against the actor's carried gear and talents (a Heavy weapon for Brace, automatic/semi-automatic capability for the burst and suppressing actions, two ready weapons or Swift/Lightning Attack for Multiple Attacks) — the grey is visible-only: nothing is locked and the click still responds.
- Unrolled actions post a lookup chat card carrying the action name as a compendium doc link, the action type and the book's terse description, so the full verbatim prose stays one click away.
- Rollable actions (Table 9-4's printed tests — Weapon Skill, Ballistic Skill, Strength and Dodge) now roll through the system's roll pipeline: the printed test difficulty (e.g. Hard (−20)) lands as a visible modifier row in the test dialog and on the card, and a follow-up chat card carries the roll result alongside the action's doc link. Opposed tests stay manual for now — the card notes that the opposing side rolls their test; no auto-comparison.
