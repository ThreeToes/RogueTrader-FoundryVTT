- The Toxic weapon quality now pulls its Toughness Test through the shared
  Test machinery: a hit that deals at least 1 point of Damage after Armour
  and Toughness Bonus reductions shows a −5-per-damage-point Toughness Test
  button on the damage card, with the penalty visible in the modifier
  breakdown.
- A failed Toxic Toughness Test points the GM at the toxin's secondary
  effects (the canonical toxin deals 1d10 Impact Damage with no reduction);
  per-toxin refinements stay GM-adjudicated.
