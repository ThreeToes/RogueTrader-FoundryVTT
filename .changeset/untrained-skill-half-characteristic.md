- Untrained basic skill tests now target HALF the governing Characteristic (round down) instead of the full characteristic with a −10 penalty (Core Rulebook p74; Dodge example p244).
- The halved value is the EFFECTIVE characteristic: characteristic modifier rows (mutations, gear, origin traits) fold in before halving with no separate breakdown row, so a −15 mutation on Agility 35 gives an untrained Dodge target of 10, not 2; trained tests keep the rows as visible funnel contributions on the effective value.
