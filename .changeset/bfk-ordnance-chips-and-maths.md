- The Ship Combat tab now renders the six Battlefleet Koronus ordnance rows (Launch Torpedoes, Torpedo Run, Reload Torpedo Tubes, Bombing Run, Assault Boat Run, Attack with Fighters) as action chips in their own "Torpedoes & Attack Craft" group.
- Dropping a ship-ordnance item onto the ship now installs it as the ship's torpedo/attack-craft stores instead of deleting it, so the ordnance rows are actually reachable.
- A failed torpedo-salvo attack now scores no hits (Battlefleet Koronus p7: one torpedo strikes only on a successful test) instead of always scoring one.
- Torpedo-run movement is now capped by the salvo's Speed as well as its remaining range.
