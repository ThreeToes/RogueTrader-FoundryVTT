- Launchers that carry no swappable ordnance (no `acceptsAmmo`) now fire standalone on their own printed profile instead of being refused as unloaded — the book treats their default ammunition as an included supply (Core Rulebook p114), not an item to load.
- The six unusual launcher rounds — Minefield, Scatter and Starflare Rounds, Cyclonic, Life-Seeker and Web Missiles — are now ordnance and can be loaded into the launcher the book names (grenade launchers also accept the Starflare Round).
- Launchers that do name an accepted ordnance kind are unchanged: an empty launcher still refuses to fire its damage roll.
